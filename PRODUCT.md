# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS assembled by a Node `build.mjs` (partials, inlined CSS and JS, icon sprite), output in `dist/`, served by Vercel with no install step. This is the house standard for Credialta Sites client sites since 2026-09 (see the repo CLAUDE.md), not a per-project choice.

## Users

People and small-business owners in Brazil who just had credit denied by a bank while their name is clean (no public negative records). They are confused and upset, already have a destination for the money (car, house, working capital, a stalled project) and arrive mostly from Meta Ads on a phone, often at night. They are trying to understand why they were refused, not shopping for a consultancy.

## Product Purpose

V8 Consultoria e Assessoria Financeira analyzes, contests and corrects wrong credit information held by banks (internal flags, bank rating, internal score) and in the Central Bank's SCR, so the client can get credit again. Success for the site: a visitor requests the free analysis (form or WhatsApp) or downloads the authorization term.

## Positioning

Post-paid: the client pays R$ 2.850 only after the credit is released (PIX, card up to 3x, or boleto 1+2). No entry fee, no analysis fee, no monthly fee. V8 corrects wrong data; it does not erase true debt ("correção, não apagamento"), which separates it from "limpa nome" schemes.

## Operating Context

The client (Augusto) works 100% digital, all of Brazil, CPF and CNPJ. Flow: sign the authorization term on gov.br, V8 queries Registrato/SCR, client receives the diagnosis, decides, signs the service contract, V8 contests within up to 45 days, client gets a completion document and 7 days to check, then pays. Documents offered for download: authorization term (PDF), commercial terms (PDF), SCR correction petition draft (DOCX).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact data still missing (WhatsApp, CNPJ, domain, hours, Instagram); e-mail `suporte@assessoriav8.com.br` came from the client's own video, to be confirmed.
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are required (the form collects CPF/CNPJ under LGPD) and do not exist yet.
- The hero promise ("você só paga depois que o crédito sair") needs the client's commercial terms (clauses 5 and 11) adjusted before launch; today the terms bill on service completion.
- Approval, limit, rate and release of credit are always the bank's decision; the site must never promise approval.

## Brand Commitments

Brand board approved 2026-09-23 (`Recursos Site/00 - IDENTIDADE VISUAL.png`): the 3D chrome emerald logo is the only 3D element; everything else is flat. Palette sampled from the logo (mint #00F9A4 as the single accent over #020D09 / #081A14). Archivo Black, Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono. The "linha da virada" (the V stroke that dips and rises past its start, with mint nodes) is the signature graphic. Voice: direct, no jargon, no guru tone, no "prezado".

## Evidence on Hand

Four client videos (vertical 9:16), four generated photos (hero desktop/mobile, credit denied, couple with house and car), the logo files and the brand board, the client's documents. No testimonials, no case numbers, no client count: V8 is a new company and none of these may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show what the bank sees: the page explains the invisible record before selling the fix.
2. The client never pays to try; every CTA stays free of pressure devices.
3. Correction, not erasure: legal grounding (CDC art. 43, LGPD art. 18) is part of the pitch, and the legal notice in the footer is not decoration.
4. Transparency over proof: with no track record yet, the documents and the post-paid model are the proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first audience on mid-range phones and mobile data: performance and legibility on dark backgrounds are part of accessibility. WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, real labels on the form.
